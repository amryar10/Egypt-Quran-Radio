# Egypt Quran Radio

A lightweight, modern live Quran radio streaming app designed with a clean, distraction-free interface.

## Features

- **Background & Screen-Off Playback:** Native Android Foreground Service keeps the live radio playing smoothly when you press Home or lock your screen, with a persistent notification and Stop button.
- **Instant Live Playback:** Simple **Play** and **Stop** controls with automatic stream fallback for uninterrupted listening.
- **Animated Sound Wave:** Smooth, volume-responsive equalizer visualizer that activates while streaming.
- **Volume Control:** Built-in slider for quick audio adjustment.
- **System Theme & Manual Toggle:** Automatically matches your device's **Light** or **Dark** mode, with a one-tap toggle button.
- **Dynamic Accent Colors:** Launches with a randomized accent color (**Red, Green, Blue, Cyan, Pink, or Yellow**) that you can switch anytime.
